## Goal

The gateway derives its per-(rail, tier) model lists itself, at each catalog refresh, from evidence in its own store — the benchmark rows its OpenRouter leg already reads, the Go price, the selected endpoints, the clean-ride rates — and git keeps only the rulings' numbers (`tiers.score`, `bands`, `cap`, `cost`). Nothing re-cuts a snapshot: no `kubectl exec` into the old proxy, no `benchmarks-snapshot.sh`, no `tiers apply`, no PR, and `TierEvidenceStale` stops being a call for hands.

The operator's rulings this Goal carries (2026-11-12), each ⚖ on the rows it decides:
- On #3231's scheduled re-cut ("it execs into deploy/openrouter-proxy and lands benchmarks.json + policy.json by PR"): "is this the best solution we can come up with? exec into a pod? Why not do it properly with interfaces, S3, database, etc?"
- On committed lists with a scheduled re-cut PR against a runtime derivation with git keeping the rulings' numbers: "I am also leaning towards runtime. But with some monitoring, S3, grafana, etc. Can all be added later when it works already."
- On what a session walks when the gateway holds no derivation — defer every session until the first derivation completes, or keep seed lists in the policy: "2 defer".
- On the budget: "Goal budgets are whatever, we dont have proper counting yet anyway - put €30 or so."

**Acid test:** replay 2026-11-01 → 2026-11-11 against fakes — the benchmark feed moves on the 8th (a model gains a newer generation, eight rows lose `intelligence`), nobody commits anything for ten days. On the 11th the lists `POST /sessions` walks are the derivation over the 11th's evidence, `GET /catalog/tiers` shows them with the evidence row behind each id, and the evidence-age gauge never passed one refresh interval. And a gateway started on an empty store answers every `POST /sessions` `defer` until its first derivation lands, then dispatches.

Design pins the children elaborate:
- **Moved, not rewritten.** The derivation is `agent_gateway.tiers` as GW-MODEL-TIERS states it today — the same bands, cap, score axis, id join, cost per clean ride, undecided and unscored rules. Only where its inputs come from and where its output goes change.
- **Shadow, then cut over.** First the gateway derives and shows (the admission still walks the policy's lists, and the view says whether the two agree); then the admission walks the derivation. Each step is one child.
- **No derivation, no dispatch** (the ruling). A rail with no derivation is held; with none on any rail every session defers. The last good derivation is kept in the store and survives a restart, as the catalog snapshots do. No seed list: a seed is a second copy that goes stale.
- **The subscription rails' candidates stay declared where they are** ⚖: for `anthropic` and `codex` the policy's `rails.<rail>.models` ids are the candidate set (tier membership and order are derived from them); the priced rails' candidates come from the two catalogs. No new policy key, so no two-PR loader dance.
- **The operator's read is not touched.** No child adds an upstream read on a subscription credential or puts an account-bound value into a committed file. The codex candidate RULE stays #3145's; a child that finds it needs such a read stops and says so.
- **The policy edit is post-launch.** The children touch `gateway/**` only and the new loader accepts the policy with and without the priced rails' lists; taking the lists, `benchmarks.json` and its mount out of `argocd/resources/agent-gateway/` happens after the image that no longer reads them is deployed (README §Test: a ConfigMap rolls before the image).
- **Later, not here** (the ruling): S3 copies of the evidence, a dashboard, alerts beyond the one that exists.

## Acceptance

1. `GET /catalog/openrouter` serves every benchmark row the leg reads, ranked or not (#3306).
2. `GET /catalog/tiers` shows, per rail and tier, the derived list, the evidence behind each id, the undecided and unscored names, and whether the list equals the one the admission walks (#3307).
3. `POST /sessions` walks the derived lists; a rail with no derivation is held and with none anywhere the answer is `defer`; the last good derivation survives a restart (#3308).
4. Nothing under `gateway/` reads `argocd/resources/agent-gateway/benchmarks.json` or the policy's priced-rail lists, `benchmarks-snapshot.sh` and the `tiers snapshot | apply | check` verbs are gone, the "⚑ gap: the snapshot is hand-refreshed" row is deleted, and `gateway_tier_evidence_age_seconds` reads the derivation's evidence (#3309).
5. Production leg: after the image bump, the post-launch change removes the `opencode` and `openrouter` lists from the deployed `policy.json`, `benchmarks.json`, its ConfigMap entry and `GATEWAY_BENCHMARKS`; the live `GET /catalog/tiers` then answers lists equal to the ones the admission walked, with no commit behind them.

## Out of scope

- The codex candidate rule and a codex catalog leg (#3145, #3296); the standing residue in #2977 (subscription price, coding/agentic axes, Go-only id spellings).
- Any change to a band, the cap, the score axis or the cost terms.
- The old proxy: its `_capability_tick` and table stay untouched; nothing under `argocd/resources/openrouter-proxy/`.
- S3, Grafana, new alerts; `.github/workflows/gateway-image.yaml`.
- An upstream read on a subscription credential; an account-bound value in a committed file.
