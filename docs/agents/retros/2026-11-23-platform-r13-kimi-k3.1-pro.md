## Findings (ranked, ≤6)
### 1. Extend r12 F1: successive specs reviews still expand the blocking set after implementation
- **Evidence:** [homelab PR #3564](https://github.com/teststuffstash/homelab/pull/3564) had seven numbered attempts over its lifetime, five green CI heads, five code-review decisions and three specs rounds. On **2026-11-20**, after the five-logic-round bound, operator grants authorized r6/r7 for specs-only findings. [oracle-fleet PR #1107](https://github.com/teststuffstash/oracle-fleet/pull/1107) used four worker rounds, six code-review and four specs verdicts, plus three body-only coordinator resolutions; its **2026-11-22** r4 addressed another R10 alignment finding after earlier R7/R2/R10 rounds. CI was predominantly green while these loops ran.
- **Mechanism:** each specs pass re-reads unchanged rows and can promote newly noticed findings to blocking, so the blocking set need not shrink. This is the existing `specs-review-expanding-scope-exhausts-round-bound` mechanism, not a new finding identity.
- **Change:** from round 2, the **specs reviewer rubric (ADR-155)** should put findings on rows unchanged since its first verdict under `Follow-ups:` unless the PR introduced a regression.
- **Expected saving:** the conservative tail is three worker rounds—#3308 r6/r7 and #1095 r4—totalling **489,355 fresh-input, 2,989,366 cached-input and 24,556 output tokens**, plus 2,650 seconds of active worker time and associated re-reviews. The two #3308 grants would save **€6–16 if batched into one sitting**; avoiding a lone summons instead saves `E/B + minutes`, with the fixed entry approximately €17–25. These worker/reviewer rides are rail 3: cash-amortized monthly-fee share and API-equivalent list×tokens are both unpriced from this bundle; marginal cash cost is approximately $0 under headroom. No rail-2 amount is claimed.

### 2. Extend r12’s shared-ownership finding: Goal fan-out still leaves acceptance contracts unowned
- **Evidence:** [Goal #3586](https://github.com/teststuffstash/homelab/issues/3586) appropriately fanned implementation into child PRs #3608/#3610/#3611/#3624/#3632: 13 CI-bearing heads, 12 green, with 11 code-review decisions. Nevertheless, [assembly PR #3637](https://github.com/teststuffstash/homelab/pull/3637) needed three code-review rounds and five CI heads. Review found failed issues not being reselected, impossible Goal-child `t0`, Argo `Error` stopping sittings, non-atomic reopen/label handling and themed-Goal `t0` remaining pending. These were selector/contract boundaries, not ordinary in-child defects. [PR #3648](https://github.com/teststuffstash/homelab/pull/3648) subsequently closed unmerged after **1,083 seconds, 173,587 fresh-input, 2,093,834 cached-input and 23,646 output tokens**, illustrating duplicate-repair exposure.
- **Mechanism:** functional child decomposition without explicit file/acceptance-row ownership lets cross-child invariants reach assembly before any child owns them.
- **Change:** in `agents/coordinator/README.md` W1 spec-gap/child-creation handling, declare the file and exact acceptance-row owner for each child; use the README’s native `blockedBy` dependency rule to serialize genuinely shared mutable artifacts.
- **Expected saving:** a bounded target is **1–2 assembly worker/review cycles per coupled Goal**, with #3648’s tokens as an upper-bound duplicate-ride opportunity, not a guaranteed saving. This is rail 3 only: cash-amortized share and API-equivalent price are unmeasured, with marginal cash approximately $0 under headroom. No operator-touch or paid-API saving is asserted.

### 3. Extend r11 F2/r12 F5: dependency review currency still follows HEAD instead of policy inputs
- **Evidence:** [oracle-fleet #1051](https://github.com/teststuffstash/oracle-fleet/pull/1051) received **14 `CHANGES_REQUESTED` reviews lifetime, nine inside this window**, followed by two approvals and merge on 2026-11-22. The one-line Prometheus test pin and decisive policy hold did not change on rebase-only heads; the arbitration comment counted 14 bot verdicts against a cap of 12. The same substance previously occurred on [sleep-tracking #218](https://github.com/teststuffstash/sleep-tracking/pull/218) and [oracle-fleet #965](https://github.com/teststuffstash/oracle-fleet/pull/965). ADR-166 subsequently removed this instance’s obsolete upper-minor cap, allowing the final approval—one favorable policy opportunity, not a trend.
- **Mechanism:** a Renovate rebase changes HEAD and re-fires review even when changed-file content, production/target pins, ADR/lens revision and the human hold are unchanged.
- **Change:** the existing **reviewer scan migration-lens rebase-debounce clause** should fingerprint that content-plus-policy tuple and stand aside while it and the live verdict remain unchanged; a policy-version or human-hold change forces one fresh evaluation.
- **Expected saving:** conservatively **eight duplicate in-window blocking reviews** on #1051 after the first in-window verdict. These are rail-3 reviewer rides: cash-amortized cost is the monthly-fee window share, normally small and marginally approximately $0 under headroom; API-equivalent list×tokens is unmeasured. No operator summons or rail-2 saving is claimed for r13.

- **Expected saving:** at most **three first-touch/model-swap rides** in this window. They are rail 3 where subscription-routed; both cash-amortized monthly-fee share and API-equivalent list×tokens are unpriced. No operator or paid-API saving is inferred. Confidence is low because `n=3` and task mix is confounded.
